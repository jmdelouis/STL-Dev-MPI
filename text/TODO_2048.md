# When the nside 2048 ensembles arrive

The paper's experiment (section 6) is drafted on the nside 512 and 1024
ensembles. The final version uses the nside 2048 ensembles of the
production chain:

| Run | Members | Where the members will be |
|---|---|---|
| hpc job 2059308 (1 H200), post-processed by 2059309 (`ens_post.slurm`: histogram matching) | 5 | `~/stl-mpi/ens/runs/n2048_2059308/synthesis_mMMM_nested.npy` (kappa) and `syn_mMMM_synthesis_nested.npy` (g) |
| Datarmor jobs 7775549 and 7775550 (2048, 100 + 100 iterations, 448 cores, queued after 7775548), from the Irene 1024 ensemble 6087067 continued by 300 iterations on Datarmor (job 7775548: 400 ranks in 10 groups of 40 on 16 nodes, Intel MPI, 259 s per evaluation); setting of Table `tab:runs` (commit 39d2f60, no Minkowski or tail term) | 10 | on Datarmor; they must reach hpc `/scratch` directly from Datarmor (never through the PC), or the scripts run on Datarmor |

The Irene jobs 6090002 (1024, +150 iterations) and 6090003 (2048) were
cancelled on 29 Oct: they duplicated the Datarmor chain. The Irene
contribution is the finished 1024 ensemble 6087067.

The 10 realizations at nside 2048 are already measured
(`/scratch/jmdeloui/paper/cache/n2048/real/r000.npz` ... `r009.npz`, job
2094597), so only the members remain.

## 1. Check the members

- every member directory holds `synthesis_mMMM_nested.npy` = kappa after
  histogram matching with r000 (`tools/gaussianize.py match
  prep/n2048/gauss_table.npz g.npy kappa.npy`; on hpc `ens_post.slurm` does
  it); the scripts read only these files;
- the run reports (`syn.json`): number of iterations and evaluations,
  `seconds_per_evaluation`, `memory_peak_bytes_per_rank`, the loss weights
  (`level_power.weight`, `marginal.weight`, `kappa`, `kurtosis`, scatter),
  `final_residual_in_ergodic_sigma`, `ensemble.per_member_in_ergodic_sigma`.

## 2. Figures and table (hpc)

```bash
# copy the current scripts (from the repository root, in WSL)
tar cz text/scripts tools/minkowski.py tools/test_minkowski.py | ssh hpc 'tar xzf - -C /scratch/jmdeloui/paper/src'
# measure the members and draw every figure at 2048 (DATARMOR_DIR: where the
# Datarmor members were put on hpc)
ssh hpc 'bash -l -s' <<'EOF'
export P=/scratch/jmdeloui/paper STEPS=2048
export RUNS2048='"hpc 5 maps=$HOME/stl-mpi/ens/runs/n2048_2059308" "Datarmor 10 maps=DATARMOR_DIR"'
export MAPS_RUN='Datarmor 10 maps=DATARMOR_DIR'
cd $P/logs && sbatch --export=ALL $P/src/text/scripts/paper_figures.slurm
EOF
# fetch
ssh hpc 'cd /scratch/jmdeloui/paper/figures && tar cf - *_n2048*' | tar xf - -C text/figures
```

The variables are exported before `sbatch --export=ALL` because
`--export=A=...,B=...` splits on commas. About 4.5 minutes per member at 6
threads, 5 at a time: 15 members in about 15 minutes, plus the figures. The
labels become the column heads of the table and the legends; keep them
short. `MAPS_RUN` picks the run shown in the map figure (default: the
last of `RUNS2048`).

Then the Minkowski functionals per level (same exports, `NSIDES=2048`
instead of `STEPS=2048`, script `mf_levels.slurm`; outputs
`mf_levels_n2048.pdf/.tex/.txt`, cached as `*_mfl.npz`).

Expected outputs of `paper_figures.slurm`: `cl_n2048.pdf/.txt`, `levels_n2048.pdf`,
`peaks_pdf_n2048.pdf`, `minkowski_n2048_s0.pdf`, `_s1.pdf`, `.txt`,
`table_zscores_n2048.tex/.txt`, `maps_mollweide_n2048.png`,
`maps_gnomonic_n2048.png`.
